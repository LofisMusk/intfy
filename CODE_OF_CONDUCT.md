# Code of Conduct

Short version: be decent to each other.

## Do

- Be friendly and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Criticize code, not people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you assume.
- Take feedback without getting defensive. Everyone ships bugs.

## Don't

- Insult, harass or threaten anyone.
- Make fun of anyone for who they are or for how much they know.
- Post someone's private info (address, email, server details) without their OK.
- Spam, troll or derail discussions.

## If something's wrong

Message [@LofisMusk](https://github.com/LofisMusk) on GitHub instead of opening a public issue. Reports are kept private.

Breaking these rules can get your comments removed, or get you blocked from the project.

This applies everywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
